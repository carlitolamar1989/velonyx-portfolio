The Velonyx AI Front Desk has two charges: a **one-time build fee** (USD 700 Essentials · 900 Growth · 1,200 Elite) and a **monthly subscription** (USD 70 · 150 · 400, plus the USD 200 AI Video add-on if chosen). Each is treated differently.

## 1. Build fee

| When you cancel | Refund |
|---|---|
| Within **48 hours** of paying **and** before kickoff (no kickoff call held, no assets received, no work started) | **Full refund** — we absorb the payment-processing fees |
| After kickoff or after any build work has started | **Non-refundable.** The engineering hours are spent the moment we start stamping and configuring your instance. You receive the work completed to date if you ask for it |
| We fail to deliver | If Velonyx cannot deliver an instance that meets the SOW's acceptance criteria within 30 days after the target date for reasons within our control, you may cancel and receive a **full refund** of the build fee |

If **you** delay the build (inputs not provided) the fee stays non-refundable; the project resumes when your inputs arrive.

## 2. Monthly subscription (+ add-ons)

- **Cancel any time, online.** From the owner portal (Settings → Subscription → Cancel) or by emailing admin@velonyxsystems.com from your account email. No phone call, no notice period.
- Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the billing period you have already paid for**. You keep full service until then. **We do not refund or prorate partial months** — you keep the full service you paid for through the end of the period.
- **Service credit.** If your instance is down for more than 72 consecutive hours for reasons within Velonyx's control (not a Twilio/Meta/Google/Anthropic outage, not your account being suspended by a carrier or platform, not non-payment), we credit that month's fee on request.
- Add-ons follow the same rule: drop them at the end of any billing period; no partial-month refunds.
- Annual prepay: full refund within 14 days of payment if the year has not started; after that, the unused whole months are refunded on cancellation, minus the discount you received (i.e., months used are re-priced at the monthly rate).

## 3. After cancellation

Your data stays exportable for 30 days, and if you have completed 12 consecutive paid months you can request the ownership transfer of your instance instead. Details are in the Terms of Service (Sections 12–13) and the Data Processing Agreement.

## 4. Chargebacks

Please email us before disputing a charge with your bank. Most billing problems are fixed within a business day. If a chargeback is filed on a charge this policy says is non-refundable, we will respond to the dispute with the signed SOW and delivery records, and the instance may be paused while the dispute is open.

## 5. How to request a refund

1. Email admin@velonyxsystems.com from your account email with your business name and what you are requesting.
2. We reply in writing within 2 business days confirming which case above applies.
3. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days (bank posting may take longer).

## 6. Where this fits

This Refund Policy is Exhibit B to the Master Services Agreement and part of the Terms of Service. If it conflicts with your signed SOW, the SOW controls for that engagement. Consumers in some jurisdictions have rights that this policy cannot limit; where that is the case, those rights apply.
