Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you pay.

Is there really nothing big to pay up front? ▾

Correct. On the monthly plan your first payment is just the monthly price. Part of each of your first 12 payments (the “build portion” — $59, $79, or $99 depending on plan) pays for building your system; the rest is the month’s service. That is why the price drops after payment twelve.

What if I cancel during the first year? ▾

Within 48 hours of your first payment and before kickoff, everything is refunded and nothing is owed. After that, cancel any time online; the unpaid build portion for the remaining months is due (for example, cancel Essentials after month 4: 8 × $59 = $472) and no further service fee is charged. After month 12, cancel any time with nothing further owed. See the Refund Policy.

Is Affirm guaranteed? ▾

No. Affirm decides eligibility, terms, and rates at checkout, and it isn’t available for every business or every purchase. If Affirm isn’t offered to you, the monthly plan gives you a payment schedule with no credit check at all — that is the point of it.

Does applying with Affirm affect my credit? ▾

Affirm explains its own checks and terms before you commit; read its disclosure at checkout — it governs, not this page. Our monthly plan involves no credit check.

What happens after month 12? ▾

Your price drops to the service price ($70 / $150 / $400 a month) and you can ask us to transfer the whole system — code, database, configuration — to accounts you control. Most clients keep us on to host, maintain, and improve it; you can also prepay a year at 10 months’ price.

How does Velonyx get paid if I use Affirm? ▾

Affirm pays Velonyx the full amount up front; you repay Affirm under the plan you chose. Your build starts as soon as the payment clears, whichever way you pay.
