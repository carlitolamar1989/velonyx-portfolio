Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you pay.

Is financing guaranteed? ▾

No. Pay-over-time options are offered by third-party providers through Stripe, at their discretion, subject to their approval and terms. We can't promise a specific option, rate, or down payment. If none is offered, you can pay the build fee in full by card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.

Does applying affect my credit? ▾

That depends on the provider and the option you choose. Each provider explains its own credit checks, fees, and any interest before you commit. Read their disclosure at checkout — it governs, not this page.

What does the $70/month cover, and can I cancel? ▾

Hosting, monitoring, security updates, support, and the AI usage included in your plan. Cancel any time from your portal or by emailing admin@velonyxsystems.com; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. See our Refund Policy and Terms.

How does Velonyx get paid if I choose pay-over-time? ▾

The provider pays Velonyx for the build; you repay the provider under the terms you accepted with them. Your project starts as soon as the payment clears, regardless of your repayment schedule.

I don't want to finance. Can I just pay? ▾

Absolutely. Most clients pay the $700 build in full at checkout. Questions? Call or text (877) 317-8643.
