Common questions

Do I really own the system?

Yes. Your content, customer data, domain, and phone number are yours from day one. After 12 monthly payments (or a paid year) we transfer the whole system — code repository, database, configuration — to accounts you control. Unlike Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, GoHighLevel, or Wix, it isn't rented forever.

Why is this so much cheaper than an agency?

One master platform, engineered once and configured for you — modern stack, refined process, no agency overhead. Same premium build, a fraction of the price. We earn over time on the monthly service, so the build is priced to get you live, not to pad a quote.

What if I cancel?

Within 48 hours of your first payment and before kickoff: everything refunded, nothing owed. After that you can cancel any time online; during the first 12 months the unpaid build portion ($59 / $79 / $99 per remaining month) is due because the system was built for you, and no further service fee is charged. After month 12, cancel any time with nothing further owed. Full details in the Refund Policy.

What does the monthly service price cover?

Hosting, SSL, backups, security updates, the AI usage included in your plan, support, and your owner portal. Essentials covers web chat and booking; Growth adds two-way texting, Instagram/Messenger DMs, and follow-ups; Elite adds the AI voice agent, WhatsApp, and a monthly AI video.
